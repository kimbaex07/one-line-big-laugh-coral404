# One Line, Big Laugh

> My dentist told me to floss the teeth I want to keep, so I flossed one tooth and named him Gregory.

Character count: 99

## Why it lands

The line opens on the most ordinary dental advice imaginable — floss the teeth you want to keep — and then takes a sharp left turn: the narrator obeys it with malicious literalness, flossing exactly one tooth. Naming him Gregory is the kicker: it turns a lazy shortcut into a tiny act of devotion, and one proper noun makes the whole image stick in your head long after the first reading.
